How the quiz works

This quiz asks what makes you feel close when your partner does it. Each statement describes a specific moment, like a photo of their lunch, a long hug, or hearing “we’re okay” after an argument, and you rate how much it sounds like you.

Your answers add up to ten preferences. You see all ten, with the answers behind each one. If several of your scores are high, the results say so instead of forcing a single type.

The ten things the quiz looks at

Inclusion
Feeling part of your partner’s everyday life through small, ordinary updates.
Feeling known
Your partner remembering your particular tastes and the things you mention in passing.
Understanding
Feeling understood about how something felt, not just what happened.
Feeling chosen
Your partner taking the initiative to spend time with you.
Practical support
Concrete help that makes your load lighter.
Reassurance
Hearing affection said out loud, including on ordinary days.
Ease
Quiet, unhurried company without needing an activity or constant conversation.
Play
Shared jokes, affectionate teasing and being silly together.
Discovery
Trying something new together and finding out what you both make of it.
Touch
Physical affection for its own sake, like long hugs and casual touches.

Take it with your partner

Your results are useful on their own, but the quiz is built for two. When you both finish, you each get a guide to the other: what may matter most to them, habits to build, something you might overlook, and one thing to try this week. Every suggestion has a version for long-distance couples too.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the relationship quiz take?

A few minutes. There are 30 short statements, and you rate how much each one sounds like you.

Is the quiz free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it is free, and you don’t need an account. Your answers stay in your browser.

How do my partner and I compare results?

Copy your result link and send it to your partner. When they finish the quiz from that link, each of you sees a guide to what matters most to the other and one thing to try this week.

How is this different from a love language quiz?

Love language quizzes sort people into a few broad categories. This quiz looks at ten specific things that can make someone feel close, such as discovery, play, quiet company and reassurance, and turns your partner’s results into concrete things you can do.

Does it work for long-distance couples?

Yes. You each take the quiz on your own device, and the partner guide includes a version of each suggestion for when you’re apart.

Is this a scientific personality test?

No. It is a self-reflection quiz, not a validated personality assessment or a compatibility score. Use it to start a conversation.
